# Políticas de la tienda (borradores)

Se pegan en **Configuración → Políticas** (Shopify las publica en `/policies/...` con el diseño del tema y el footer enlaza a ellas solas).

- **Privacy policy:** ya existe (la genera Shopify). Revisarla y cambiar el nombre de la tienda "spanish" por "The Spanish Store" en Configuración → General.
- **Terms of service:** usar el botón "Crear a partir de plantilla" de Shopify y revisarlo con un asesor legal.
- **Shipping policy** y **Refund policy:** borradores de abajo, coherentes con el FAQ, el carrito y la ficha (envío gratis desde 80 CAD, Express 5 CAD, corte 14:00, devoluciones 30 días). Revisar con las condiciones reales antes de publicar.

## Shipping policy

```html
<h2>Where we ship</h2>
<p>We ship to all provinces and territories of Canada.</p>

<h2>Processing time</h2>
<p>Orders placed before 14:00 (Eastern Time) from Monday to Friday are dispatched the same business day. Orders placed after that time, on weekends or on public holidays are dispatched the next business day.</p>

<h2>Shipping methods and costs</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Free shipping</strong> on orders over 80 CAD: delivery in 7–20 business days.</li>
  <li><strong>Express shipping</strong>: 5 CAD, delivery in 1–3 business days.</li>
</ul>
<p>The available methods and the final cost are shown at checkout before you pay.</p>

<h2>Chilled products</h2>
<p>Cheese, charcuterie and other chilled products travel in thermal packaging with cold packs so they arrive in perfect condition. Please store them in the fridge as soon as you receive them.</p>

<h2>Tracking</h2>
<p>When your order is dispatched you will receive an email with the tracking number and a link to follow your delivery.</p>

<h2>Problems with your delivery</h2>
<p>If your parcel is late, lost or arrives damaged, contact us at support@spanishstore.com within 48 hours of delivery and we will help you.</p>
```

## Refund policy

```html
<h2>Returns</h2>
<p>You can return unopened, non-perishable products in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery. To start a return, email us at support@spanishstore.com with your order number and the products you want to return.</p>

<h2>Products that can't be returned</h2>
<p>For food safety reasons we can't accept returns of chilled or fresh products (such as cheese and sliced charcuterie), opened products or gift cards, unless they arrive damaged or in poor condition.</p>

<h2>Damaged or incorrect items</h2>
<p>If something arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, write to us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, including shipping.</p>

<h2>Return shipping</h2>
<p>Unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect, return shipping costs are paid by the customer.</p>

<h2>Refunds</h2>
<p>Once we receive and check your return, we will email you to confirm the refund. It will be issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.</p>

<h2>Exchanges</h2>
<p>The fastest way to get a different product is to return the one you have and place a new order.</p>
```
